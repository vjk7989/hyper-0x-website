HyperRun is a first-person shooter, or FPS. In an FPS, the player sees the game through the eyes of a character. HyperRun is a realistic FPS. Players earn money when they play and when they win.

Fans also take part. Fans predict the results of matches and players. A fan earns when a prediction is correct. Fans can also back good players. The good players then receive sponsorship.

## How it works

1. **A player joins a match.** The player plays a realistic FPS match.
2. **Fans make predictions.** Fans predict the outcome of matches and players. They can back players they believe in.
3. **The game signs the result.** When the match ends, the game signs the result. Nobody can change the result after the game signs it.
4. **A rewards contract pays out.** A smart contract on Hyper 0x settles the predictions. It then pays the winners.
5. **The money reaches a wallet.** Players and fans receive their money in an in-game wallet. The wallet uses the Hyper Wallet SDK.

## Why results can be trusted

The game signs every result before any payout happens. The game then records the signed result on Hyper 0x. A player, a fan or the studio cannot change the record after the game signs it.
