This page describes the features of HyperRun. Each section explains one feature.

## Earn by playing

Players earn money when they play. They earn more when they win. The earnings reach an in-game wallet.

## In-game predictions

Fans predict the results of matches and of individual players. A prediction is a guess about what will happen. When a prediction is correct, the fan earns. When it is wrong, the fan does not earn.

## Player sponsorship

Fans can back players through their predictions. Good players receive sponsorship from the fans who back them. This gives strong players a way to earn support from their fans.

## Signed match results

The game signs every result before any payout. The signature proves who made the record. Nobody can change a result after the game signs it. This keeps the game fair for players and fans.

## Rewards contract

A smart contract on Hyper 0x settles the predictions. It then pays the winners. A smart contract runs by itself when its conditions are true.

## In-game wallet

Players and fans receive a wallet inside the game. The wallet uses the Hyper Wallet SDK. A user can hold and move funds without a separate wallet app.
