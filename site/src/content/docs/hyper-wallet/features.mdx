This page lists the main features of Hyper Wallet. Each section explains one feature in simple terms.

## Multi-chain and non-custodial

Hyper Wallet works with Bitcoin, Litecoin, Bitcoin Cash, Dash, Ethereum and other EVM chains. It also works with Solana, Tron, TON, XRP, Stellar and Sui. Your keys stay on your device. The company does not hold your funds. A business that needs custody can add it through the SDKs.

## Swap and trade in one place

You can swap from any coin page. The app picks a route with a good price. Each swap has MEV protection, which limits how a trader can move a trade ahead of yours. Both sides of the swap settle at the same time.

## Perps, pre-IPO and index funds

Perpetual futures are contracts that have no fixed end date. You can trade them in the app. You can also invest in tokenized pre-IPO stocks, which are shares before a public listing. Index funds hold a basket of assets, so one purchase spreads your money across many assets.

## Markets beyond coins

The app shows more than coins. You can see pump.fun tokens, which are tokens from a popular token launch site. You can see tokenized assets such as gold and stocks. You can also see Polymarket prediction markets, where people bet on the outcome of events.

## Deposit from exchanges

You can add funds from Binance or Coinbase. Open a coin page and choose the deposit option. The app fills in the address for you. You do not need to copy the address by hand.

## dApps and NFTs

A dApp is an app that runs on a blockchain. You can connect the wallet to dApps through WalletConnect. WalletConnect is a standard that links a wallet to another app. You can also keep your NFTs next to your coins. An NFT is a unique digital item.

## On-chain order book

The order book runs inside the Hyper 0x consensus. Every validator works out the same fills. So no single party can trade ahead of an order.

## One fee, not two

You pay one transaction cost. You do not pay an exchange fee and then a wallet fee. This keeps the cost easy to see.

## Simple status

Each transaction shows one of three states: pending, confirmed or failed. You do not need to compare records across different apps.

## White-label SDKs

The SDKs let a business put a wallet, payments and a payment gateway into its own app or game. The business keeps its own brand. The SDKs also connect to bank ledgers and core banking APIs. A core banking API is the interface that a bank uses to run its main systems.
