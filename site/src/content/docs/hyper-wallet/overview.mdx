Hyper Wallet is a crypto wallet and exchange in one app. A crypto wallet holds digital coins and tokens. An exchange lets you trade them. With Hyper Wallet, you hold, swap and trade in one place. The app works across many blockchains.

Hyper Wallet is **non-custodial**. This means the company does not hold your keys. Your private keys stay on your own device. A private key is the secret code that approves a transfer.

Businesses can also use the same wallet as a set of SDKs. An SDK, or software development kit, is a set of tools for developers. Banks, fintechs and app makers use the SDKs to launch their own wallet, neobank or payment gateway. A neobank is a bank that works only in an app. A payment gateway takes payments for a business.

## How it works

1. **Your keys stay on your device.** You hold the keys. The company cannot move your funds.
2. **You pick a coin or asset.** Each coin page shows the asset and its markets.
3. **You swap or trade.** The app finds a route for the best price. Each swap has MEV protection. MEV, or maximal extractable value, is the profit that some traders take by moving their trade ahead of yours.
4. **Both sides settle together.** A trade settles in one step. Either both sides settle, or neither side does.
5. **You see a simple status.** The status is pending, confirmed or failed.

## What you can hold and trade

- Coins on Bitcoin, Ethereum and other EVM chains, Solana, Tron, TON, XRP, Stellar and Sui. An EVM chain runs the Ethereum Virtual Machine, the same program that Ethereum runs.
- Tokenized assets, such as gold and stocks. A tokenized asset is a digital token that stands for a real asset.
- Perpetual futures, pre-IPO stocks and index funds. These are investment products, and each one carries its own risk.
- Prediction markets from Polymarket.

## Who uses the SDKs

- **Banks and fintechs** add a wallet, payments and a payment gateway with their own brand.
- **Game studios** add an in-game wallet.
- **App makers** let their users hold and move coins without a separate app.
