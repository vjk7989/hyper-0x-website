This page describes the features of H0X Pay. Each section explains one part of the product.

## Investment plans

You set an investment plan one time. For example, the plan can take a small percentage of every payment. Your portfolio then grows each time you spend.

## Tokenized assets

H0X Pay starts with tokenized gold. Tokenized gold is a digital token backed by real gold. Index funds, tokenized stocks and tokenized bonds follow. The app selects the assets for each market.

## Crypto payments

You pay and get paid with links, QR codes and invoices. A QR code is a square code that a phone camera can read. Each payment settles on Hyper 0x.

## Roadmap

H0X Pay grows in three phases.

1. **Phase 1, gold.** You buy tokenized gold in small parts from everyday payments.
2. **Phase 2, tokenized assets.** The app adds index funds, tokenized stocks and bonds.
3. **Phase 3, payments.** You make and receive crypto payments from the same app.
