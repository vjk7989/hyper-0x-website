H0X Pay lets you pay and get paid in crypto. A crypto payment uses digital coins instead of bank cards. H0X Pay also puts a small part of every payment into tokenized assets. A tokenized asset is a digital token that stands for a real asset. The first asset is tokenized gold.

Every payment settles on Hyper 0x. Settlement is the final step, when the money moves and the record is final.

## How it works

1. **You pay as usual.** You pay a merchant with H0X Pay. The merchant receives the payment at once.
2. **Your plan sets a slice.** Your investment plan sets a small part of the payment. For example, 2 percent of a $40 payment is $0.80.
3. **The slice buys gold.** The slice buys tokenized gold at the best price that the order book shows.
4. **The purchase settles.** The purchase settles on Hyper 0x. Your gold balance then grows.

The plan runs each time you spend. You set it one time. You do not need to buy assets by hand.
